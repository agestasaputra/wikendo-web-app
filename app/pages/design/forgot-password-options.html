20 Pilihan Penempatan Button “Lupa Password?”

Setiap kartu menampilkan markup HTML minimal yang dapat dipakai langsung pada LoginPage.vue. Semua contoh menggunakan kelas yang sudah ada (Tailwind‑like) atau CSS di atas.

1. Di bawah field password, rata kanan (default)

<PasswordField v-model="password" />
<div class="text-right mt-1">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
</div>

2. Di sebelah kanan ikon mata (toggle)

👁️ 
<div class="flex items-center">
  <PasswordField v-model="password" />
  <span @click="toggle">👁️</span>
  <button class="btn small" @click="handleForgot">Lupa?</button>
</div>

3. Placeholder dalam field password (klik membuka modal)

<PasswordField v-model="password" placeholder="Lupa Password?" @focus="handleForgot" />

4. Di atas form, rata kanan

<div class="text-right">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
</div>
<PasswordField v-model="password" />

5. Footer form, di atas tombol Masuk

<PasswordField v-model="password" />
<div class="text-right mt-1">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
</div>
<button class="btn-primary">Masuk →</button>

6. Floating Action Button (FAB) kanan‑bawah form

<div class="relative">
  <PasswordField v-model="password" />
  <button class="fab" @click="handleForgot" title="Lupa Password?">?</button>
</div>

7. Button berwarna abu‑abu dengan border

<PasswordField v-model="password" />
<button class="btn" style="border:1px solid #bbb;" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>

8. Di sebelah tombol Masuk, kiri

<PasswordField v-model="password" />
<div class="flex justify-between">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa?</button>
  <button class="btn-primary">Masuk →</button>
</div>

9. Overlay modal saat focus pada password

Forgot your password?

<PasswordField v-model="password" @focus="showModal = true" />
<Modal v-if="showModal" @close="showModal = false">
  <button @click="handleForgot">Kirim Link Reset</button>
</Modal>

10. Inline di samping label “Password”

<label>Password</label>
<button class="btn small" @click="handleForgot">Lupa?</button>

11. Badge tipis berwarna lime di atas field

Lupa? 
<div class="relative">
  <span class="badge" @click="handleForgot">Lupa?</span>
  <PasswordField v-model="password" />
</div>

12. Fixed position di kanan‑bawah viewport

<button class="fixed bottom-5 right-5 btn-primary" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>

13. Ikon pertanyaan “?” di kanan field

❓ 
<div class="relative">
  <PasswordField v-model="password" class="pr-8" />
  <span class="icon" @click="handleForgot">❓</span>
</div>

14. Dropdown “More” di atas form

<select @change="if($event.target.value==='forgot') handleForgot()">
  <option disabled selected>-- Pilih Aksi --</option>
  <option value="forgot">Lupa Password?</option>
</select>

15. Di bawah “Daftar” (bersamaan dengan CTA register)

Belum punya akun? Daftar

Lupa Password? 

<p class="right">Belum punya akun? <a href="/register">Daftar</a></p>
<p class="right"><a href="#" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</a></p>

16. Sebagai teks besar di bawah banner quota

<div class="right">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
</div>

17. Placeholder di field email (bukan password)

<input type="email" placeholder="email@domain.com (Lupa?)" @focus="handleForgot" />

18. Ikon lock terbuka di kanan field

🔓 
<div class="relative">
  <PasswordField v-model="password" class="pr-8" />
  <span @click="handleForgot">🔓</span>
</div>

19. Button transparan di bagian atas form

<button class="btn" style="background:transparent;" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
<PasswordField v-model="password" />

20. Di bawah banner quota (di akhir layout)

<div class="quota-banner">…</div>
<div class="right">
  <button class="btn" @click="handleForgot">Lupa Password?</button>
</div>
